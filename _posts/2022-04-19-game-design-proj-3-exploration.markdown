EECS494

Game Design, University of Michigan


<h2>Task</h2>

![Screenshot of Coconaut](/images/coconaut_screenshot_4.png)

For the capstone of EECS494: Game Design at the University of Michigan, we were tasked with creating a game for an audience and use real-world iterative development with play testers, project management, and even a Jira board. We were doing the real deal. 

We had a blank sheet with one rule - everything has to be made by us (except for music but we have support of a local videogame music club)

<h2>Design</h2>

![Screenshot of Coconaut](/images/coconaut_screenshot_2.png)

The concept started from the idea for a platformer because we all loved the classic games like Mario64. However, making a good platformer is not novel enough to capture everyone's attention. We had concepts of like survival games like Rust where you craft and build. But this would be too complex to complete for a team of three in four weeks. Some small sidetalk revealed we all agree that the puzzle rooms from the *Zelda: Breath of the Wild* are one of the best parts of the game. Hmm...

Why not take concpets from each we like? 

Thus we decided on the concept for a puzzle platformer with an inventory and crafting system to get over hurdles!
It was originally going to have a survival story like being lost in the woods and making your way home but we wanted it to be more fun and worry less about graphics. I don't quite remember how but a coconut desiring to be a curry seemed like a great idea for the story too (maybe we were hungry?). Regardless, Coconaut was born.

<h2>Gameplay</h2>

![Screenshot of Coconaut](/images/coconaut_screenshot_3.png)

<h3>Platforming and Puzzles</h3>

- The game revolves around getting to your destintation at the end of the level but many obstacles lie in your way
    - Jump with stick 
    - Climb with ropes
    - Craft bridges and cross them!
- The levels have many ways to reach the finish; keep looking for different ways!
    - Even some where you seem like you're going backwards :)

<h3>Enemies and Health</h3>

- You have health, so tread carefully
    - Don't fall or leap too far! You'll take damage   
- Crabs can attack you. Watch out!
    - You can trick them into falling over edges 

<h3>Inventory and Crafting</h3> 

- You have an inventory with either scrollwheel or arrow keys to select item
    - Different items allow different abilities!
- Don't forget to pickup and place - you never know what you may need for later


![Screenshot of Coconaut](/images/coconaut_gif.gif)
*Note that inventory can transfer levels too!*

<h3> Story and Stages </h3>
- The story is simple. You must fullfil your desire to become a delicious curry. 
    - Your Palm Mother compels you
- Different stages as you catch boats to your final destination
    - Cutscenes!

<h4>Controller support too!</h4>
